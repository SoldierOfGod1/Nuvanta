## The plan in one paragraph

Drop the 3-niche, 9-SKU launch. Pick one product (Power Bank 20,000mAh).
Run 5 customer interviews before any spend. Reframe Nuvanta as the SA
Resilience Brand (power, then water, then security, then comms). Ship a
single-product wedge in 7 days, R3k ad budget, hard kill rule. Either find
30 buyers or get a clear no, then plan Phase 2 with real data.
