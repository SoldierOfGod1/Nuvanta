## CRITICAL

### C1. No customer-validation gate before spend
The plan goes idea -> build -> paid ads in 14 days with zero validation in week 0.
**Fix:** Add Phase 0 gate. No ads until 5 user interviews logged.

### C2. Margin math is gross, not net
Plan reports gross margins (50-65%). Misses: ad CPA (~R100-R180 SA 2026),
platform fees (3-4%), Shopify (R580/mo), return rate (8-12%), chargebacks
(1-2%), support labor.
**Real net contribution margin per unit: ~20-28%, not 50-65%.**
**Fix:** Redo unit economics with full CAC + fee + return loss model.

### C3. No defined CAC ceiling / ad kill rule
Target "CPA R80-R150" with no rule for what happens at R220 CPA.
**Fix:** Kill rule = "if CPA > 1.5x sell-price x 0.4 by day 3, kill creative or product."

## HIGH

### H1. Three-niche launch contradicts "ONE winning product" rule
Plan's own closing rule says "ONE product first, ONE niche angle" but proposes
9 SKUs across 3 niches in week 1. Internal contradiction.
**Fix:** Delete the 9-SKU launch list. Keep ONE. Add others post-winner.

### H2. No data tracking plan
Missing: GA4, TikTok Pixel, Meta Pixel events, attribution, abandoned cart flow,
post-purchase sequence.
**Fix:** Confirm 5 events firing before launch (PageView, ViewProduct, AddToCart,
InitiateCheckout, Purchase).

### H3. Returns policy is one bullet
"Easy returns" without specifying: who pays return shipping, restocking fee,
refund timeline, supplier dispute handling. SA CPA mandates 7-day cooling-off
on distance sales.
**Fix:** Write CPA-compliant returns policy before listing. Footer-linked.

### H4. WhatsApp support is a labor trap
With one founder, 50 tickets/day kills the founder.
**Fix:** Scripted auto-responder + business hours + 24hr response cap + escalation rules.

### H5. No legal entity / compliance section
Trading entity (Pty/sole prop), VAT registration, POPIA compliance, business
bank account, T&Cs. None mentioned. PayFast onboarding will block without these.
**Fix:** Confirm legal stack before any payment gateway integration.
