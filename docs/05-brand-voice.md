# Brand Voice — Nuvanta

Generated 2026-05-04. Vision D — Modern Lifestyle & Essentials.

This is the first draft of brand voice. Override anything that doesn't feel
right. Voice is taste, taste is a decision — make it.

## Brand promise (one sentence)

**"Hand-picked essentials for South Africans who'd rather own one good
thing than scroll past a thousand bad ones."**

This sentence drives every page, every ad, every email. If a piece of copy
contradicts this sentence, kill the copy.

## Voice principles

1. **Curated, not cluttered.** We picked 50 things. You don't have to
   scroll 10,000.
2. **Confident, not loud.** No ALL CAPS. No exclamation parties. We don't
   shout because we don't need to.
3. **Warm, not cute.** South African, not American-TikTok cosplay. We
   sound like a trusted friend who got into the right things first.
4. **Plainspoken, not jargon.** "Charges your phone for two days" beats
   "20,000mAh ultra-capacity solution."
5. **Honest about what we're not.** We're not the cheapest. We're not the
   fastest. We're the ones you trust.

## Visual direction

### Palette
- **Background:** off-white (#F7F4EE) — warmer than pure white, easier on eye
- **Text:** charcoal (#1F1F1B) — softer than black
- **Accent:** clay terracotta (#C46A4D) OR deep olive (#5B6B3A) — pick one,
  use sparingly (CTA only, not headers)

### Typography
- **Display / headers:** one serif — Tiempos, Cormorant Garamond, or Söhne Mono
- **Body / UI:** one sans — Inter, Söhne, or Geist
- Two fonts only. Three is clutter.

### Photography
- Natural light. Real SA homes. Real SA faces.
- No AI mockups. No glossy studio shots. No men with blindingly white teeth.
- Show product in context — on a kitchen counter, in a backpack, on a desk.
- Founder appears in at least one photo. Builds trust.

### What to avoid
- Gradients, neon, drop shadows
- Shiny "BUY NOW" badges
- Countdown timers, fake scarcity ("Only 3 left!")
- Multiple exclamation points!!!
- Stock-photo white people in suits high-fiving

## Product naming convention

- Each SKU gets a **Nuvanta-given name first, technical name in subtitle.**
- Example: "**Nuvanta Daily Power Bank** — 20,000mAh, triple-port, fits a
  back pocket."
- Not: "20000mAh Ultra-Fast Triple-Port USB-C Power Bank Plus Premium"
- The Nuvanta name is the brand asset. The technical name is the spec sheet.

## Tone examples (write everything in this voice)

| Hero | "WELCOME TO NUVANTA — SHOP NOW!!" | "Two days of phone. One charger." |

| Returns | "All sales final" | "Try it for 7 days. If it's not right, we'll refund — and pay return shipping." |
| About us | "We are passionate about innovation" | "Two of us in Joburg, picking gear we'd actually use." |

## Voice in 3 sentences

If you can't write a paragraph that sounds like Nuvanta yet, write three:

> We hand-pick gear that earns its place in your day. We tell you when
> it's worth the money and when it isn't. We'd rather sell you one good
> thing than ten you'll forget by next month.

Use this paragraph (or some version of it) on the About page, in welcome
emails, and in the founder bio. Repeat it until customers can finish the
sentences themselves.
