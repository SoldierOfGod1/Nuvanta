## Below the fold #5: FAQ

**Is this airline-safe?**
Yes. 74Wh is under the 100Wh airline carry-on limit. Take it on flights
without a fuss.

**How fast does it charge my phone?**
Modern phones (iPhone 12+, Samsung S20+) reach 50% in about 30 minutes
on the USB-C PD port. Older phones charge slower.

**Will it work during load-shedding?**
Yes. Charge it overnight when grid is on, use it during outage. One full
charge runs an iPhone for 2 days of moderate use, or a Wi-Fi router for
about 4-5 hours.

**Why Nuvanta instead of Takealot?**
Takealot has 200+ power banks. We have one we tested ourselves and stand
behind. If you'd rather scroll, Takealot is fine. If you'd rather we
picked, we're here.

**What if it breaks?**
Email orders@nuvanta.co.za with your order number. Replacement ships
within 3 business days for any manufacturing defect in the first 12 months.
