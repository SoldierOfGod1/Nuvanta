# Ad Creative Briefs — Phase 1 Wedge

Generated 2026-05-04. Power Bank 20,000mAh. R3,000 budget. 7-day window.

Three Meta creatives, three TikTok creatives. One angle per creative —
don't muddy the test by combining multiple value props in one ad.

Kill rule: target CPA <= R150, abort at R250 (see docs/01-office-hours-design.md).

---

## Targeting (both platforms)

- **Geography:** South Africa, urban centres (Joburg, Cape Town, Durban,
  Pretoria, Port Elizabeth, Bloem) — exclude rural to control delivery cost
- **Age:** 25-38 (Vision D core)
- **Gender:** all (don't pre-narrow on gender; let the algorithm find)
- **Interests (Meta):** load-shedding, Eskom, smartphone accessories,
  Takealot, Superbalist (lookalike behaviour)
- **TikTok:** broad with lifestyle interest layer; let pixel optimise

Three audiences per platform: (1) interest-based, (2) lookalike of
website-visitors (will start empty — bridge with engaged-with-page lookalike
on day 3), (3) broad (no interest layer — TikTok especially likes broad).

## Production notes

- **All footage SA-native.** No stock footage. No US-accent voice-overs.
- **Founder appears in at least one** (Meta C and TikTok B). Founder
  identity = trust.
- **No fake reviews on screen.** No "TRUSTED BY 10,000" overlays.
- **Filming budget:** iPhone 13+ shoots all 6 ads. Don't outsource.
  Spend the savings on more ad budget.

---

## Test plan

**Day 1:** Launch all 6 creatives, R500 budget across them. Watch CTR.
**Day 2:** Kill bottom 2 (CTR < 0.8%). Reallocate budget to top 4.
**Day 3:** First CPA reading. Anything > R250 CPA = kill.
**Day 4-7:** Scale top performer 2-3x daily, watch CPA stays < R250.
**Day 7 review:** 30 sales = winner. < 10 sales = product is wrong, not
ads. Pivot wedge SKU.

---

## What we're NOT testing in week 1

- Different prices (price test = week 3)
- Different products (Phase 1 is one product, full stop)
- Email retargeting (no list yet)
- Dynamic Product Ads (single SKU, no point)
- Creator partnerships (Phase 2)

The only question being answered in week 1 is: **does anyone in SA buy
this Power Bank from Nuvanta at R499 with our copy?** Yes or no. Move on.
