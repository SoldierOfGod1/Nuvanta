## 1. Returns & Refunds Policy

### Headline
**Try it for 7 days. We've got you covered.**

### Body

**Your 7-day cooling-off right (ECT Act Section 44).**
Because you bought from us online, you have the right to return any
product within 7 days of receiving it, for any reason — even if you
just changed your mind. We'll refund the full amount you paid,
including the original delivery fee.

**Who pays for return shipping?**
We do. For cooling-off returns, Nuvanta covers return-courier costs.
Email orders@nuvanta.co.za with your order number and we'll send a
return-collection booking. You don't arrange the courier yourself.

**Condition of returned goods.**
Product must be in original packaging, with all accessories, and not
visibly used (charging it once to test is fine; using it as your daily
power bank for a week is not). Items returned in unsellable condition
may be refunded at a reduced amount.

**Refund timeline.**
Once we receive and inspect the product (1-2 business days after the
courier returns it to us), your refund is processed within **5 business
days** to the original payment method. PayFast and Ozow refunds
typically land in 2-3 working days after we initiate.

**Defective products (CPA Section 56 — 6-month implied warranty).**
If the product is defective, doesn't do what we said it would, or
breaks within 6 months of normal use, you can choose:
- Repair, OR
- Replacement, OR
- Full refund

This is your statutory right under the CPA — it stands in addition to
the manufacturer's warranty.

**Manufacturer warranty.**
The Nuvanta Daily Power Bank carries a 12-month manufacturer warranty
covering defects in materials or workmanship. Damage from drops,
liquid, fire, modification, or use with non-standard cables is not
covered.

**What's NOT returnable.**
- Items you've owned more than 7 days (unless defective — see CPA above)
- Items damaged through misuse
- Gift cards / store credit

**How to start a return.**
Email **orders@nuvanta.co.za** with:
- Order number
- Reason for return (one line is fine)
- Photos if the item is defective

We'll respond within 1 business day with the next steps.

Regulator at https://inforegulator.org.za.
