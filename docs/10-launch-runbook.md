# 7-Day Launch Runbook — Phase 1 Wedge

Generated 2026-05-04. Power Bank 20,000mAh. R3,000 budget. 7 days.

This is the operational script. Print it, tape it next to your screen,
follow it. Small daily discipline beats clever strategy.

---

## Day 0 — Launch eve

**Goal:** every gate cleared, ad creatives loaded, pixels firing,
support rehearsed. No spend yet.

### Morning (90 min)
- [ ] Final read-through of /docs/06-product-page.md, paste copy into Shopify
- [ ] Upload product photos (4 minimum, hero + 3 detail)
- [ ] Set price R499, VAT-inclusive, stock = "ships in 2 days"
- [ ] Connect PayFast + Ozow, run R1 test transaction with own card
- [ ] Verify order email lands in inbox within 2 min

### Afternoon (90 min)
- [ ] Run full pre-launch verification from /docs/09-tracking-setup.md §6
- [ ] Cookie banner + privacy + T&Cs + returns policy live, footer-linked
- [ ] WhatsApp Business set up: business hours, auto-reply, 5 quick-reply scripts
- [ ] orders@nuvanta.co.za, privacy@nuvanta.co.za live and forwarding to founder
- [ ] Refund a test order end-to-end via PayFast (not just create — actually refund)

### Evening (60 min)
- [ ] Upload all 6 ad creatives to Meta + TikTok ad managers (paused state)
- [ ] Verify UTMs on every ad link match the UTM tracker sheet
- [ ] Pre-fill Day 1 row in Nuvanta Daily KPI sheet
- [ ] Founder writes the actual founder note from a real product test
      *(if you haven't tested it yet, do not launch — re-read /docs/06-product-page.md)*

### Sleep
- Go to bed before midnight. Day 1 starts at 08:00.

## Day 1 — Launch

**Budget allocation:** R500 across 6 creatives = ~R83 per creative.
**Goal:** Initial reads on CTR. Don't expect sales today.

### 08:00 (15 min)
- [ ] Activate all 6 ads in Meta + TikTok
- [ ] Confirm both accounts show "Active — Learning" status
- [ ] Post one organic launch post (IG + TikTok), share to personal stories

### 13:00 (10 min) — Lunch check
- [ ] Spend on track? (~R150-R200 spent by now)
- [ ] Any creative with CTR < 0.5%? Note for evening kill
- [ ] Any rejection from Meta/TikTok ad review? Resubmit if so

### 21:00 (20 min) — Evening review
- [ ] Log Day 1 KPIs in the sheet
- [ ] **Kill any creative with CTR < 0.8% or 0 link clicks**
- [ ] Reallocate killed budget to top-2 CTR creatives
- [ ] Sales today: realistically 0-2. Don't panic.
- [ ] Note one observation: which hook resonated most?

**Trigger: if R200 spent and 0 link clicks on any platform → ad is broken.
Pause platform, double-check pixel + targeting before resuming.**

## Day 2 — First signal

**Budget:** R450. Concentrated on day-1 winners.

### 08:00 (10 min)
- [ ] Read overnight performance
- [ ] Promote any creator/IG comments that engaged organically

### 13:00 (10 min)
- [ ] Spend tracking? CTR holding from day 1?
- [ ] First sales coming in (if at all)? Confirm order email + WhatsApp confirmation flow

### 21:00 (20 min)
- [ ] Log Day 2 KPIs
- [ ] Kill bottom 2 of remaining creatives (so 4 → 2 by tomorrow)
- [ ] First CPA reading available — note even if noisy
- [ ] **If 0 sales by end of Day 2 with > R600 spent** → urgent diagnostic:
  - Pixel firing? (Test Events check)
  - Cart abandonment data? (Shopify → Analytics)
  - Ad copy contradicts product page? (mismatch between promise and landing)

## Day 3 — Decision day

**Budget:** R450.
**Rule:** Hard CPA reading. Anything > R250 = kill.

### 08:00 (15 min)
- [ ] Calculate CPA = total spend Day 1-2 ÷ Shopify orders
- [ ] **If CPA > R250 → kill the worst-performing creative permanently**
- [ ] Scale top creative 1.5x daily budget (Meta + TikTok)

### 13:00 (10 min)
- [ ] Stable spend pace? Any algorithmic delivery issues?
- [ ] First customer feedback in WhatsApp? Acknowledge within 2 hrs.

### 21:00 (25 min)
- [ ] Log Day 3 KPIs
- [ ] Email first 3 customers (if any): "What almost stopped you from
      buying?" — single question, no marketing
- [ ] Compare Meta CPA vs TikTok CPA — reallocate budget toward winner

**Trigger: 1 sale by Day 3 means signal is weak but alive. Keep going.**
**Trigger: 0 sales by Day 3 with > R1,000 spent → preview kill criteria for Day 7.**

### 13:00 (10 min)

### 21:00 (20 min)

  - On pace for 15-29? Sustainable. Hold scale.

- [ ] If a customer posted on socials, repost with permission

### 13:00 (10 min)
- [ ] Mid-day check, kill any creative now > R250 CPA

### 21:00 (15 min)
- [ ] Log Day 6 KPIs
- [ ] Forecast Day 7 final number based on Day 1-6 trajectory

## Day 7 — Read the verdict

**Budget:** R250.
**Goal:** Hold position, capture all data, prepare decision.

### 08:00 (10 min)
- [ ] Last full day of spend at scaled level
- [ ] Begin exporting: Shopify orders CSV, Meta + TikTok report CSVs, GA4 export

### 21:00 (45 min) — The verdict review
- [ ] Total spend (target ~R3,000)
- [ ] Total Shopify orders (target 30+)
- [ ] Final CPA = total spend ÷ total Shopify orders
- [ ] Net contribution = orders × R72 - spend (using best-case math)
- [ ] Refund/return rate so far
- [ ] First-week NPS (from customer surveys, even informal)

### Decision tree
- **30+ orders, CPA <= R150** → **WIN.** Plan Phase 2 (Smart Electronics
  depth — add 4-6 SKUs, scale ad budget to R10-15k/mo, formalize creative
  pipeline).
- **15-29 orders, CPA R150-R250** → **INCONCLUSIVE.** Run another 7 days
  with refreshed creative + AOV-lifting bundle. Same SKU.
- **0-14 orders, CPA > R250** → **PRODUCT IS WRONG.** Pivot wedge to
  higher-margin SKU (Mini UPS R349 gross or Smart Electronics bundle).
  Re-test for another 7 days at R3k.
- **0 orders, all CPA → infinity** → **PIVOT VISION.** Re-evaluate Vision D
  itself. Possibly Vision A or B was the correct path.

## Standing rules across all 7 days

1. **Reply to every WhatsApp message within 2 business hours.**
2. **Don't add new creatives mid-week.** Run what you launched. Iterate at
   the week boundary.
3. **Don't change the price.** Price tests are week 3+, not week 1.
4. **Don't add a second product.** Phase 1 is single-SKU. Resist the urge.
5. **Log KPIs every day before sleep.** Skipping a day = decisions made on
   memory not data, and memory is wrong.
6. **No discounts.** Discounts in week 1 train customers to wait for sales
   forever. The brand is "curated quality at a fair price," not "regularly
   marked-down dropship."

---

## Founder discipline

This week is a 7-day sprint. No new ideas. No "what if we also..."
No tinkering. The goal is to read the demand signal cleanly. Every change
to the experiment makes the signal noisier.

If you have a great idea on Day 3, write it in a "Phase 2" notes file and
move on. The cost of that idea waiting 5 days is zero. The cost of
muddying the test is R3k of bad data.

---

## What "good" looks like at end of Day 7

You can answer all of these:
- "Our CPA on the winning creative was R___."
- "Our return rate was ___%."
- "Our customers are typically ___ (age, geography, income proxy)."
- "The winning creative angle was ___."
- "The biggest customer objection was ___ (from Day 3 emails)."
- "We have ___ email subscribers from buyers."
- "Phase 2 SKUs to test based on this week's learnings: ___."

If you can answer all seven, the wedge succeeded — even if sales were
fewer than 30. Knowledge is the deliverable. Sales are the proof.
