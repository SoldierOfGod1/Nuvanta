# Customer Support Playbook — Nuvanta

Generated 2026-05-04. WhatsApp + email support for Phase 1.

Founder is the support team in Phase 1. This playbook keeps you fast,
consistent, and from saying something on a bad day that ends up on
SA Twitter.

---

## 1. Channel hierarchy

| Channel | Use for | SLA |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp Business** | Pre-sale questions, order tracking, urgent issues | 2 business hours |
| **Email (orders@)** | Anything with attachments, formal complaints, refund requests | 24 hours |
| **Instagram / TikTok DMs** | Public-facing first contact, then move to WhatsApp | 4 hours acknowledged |
| **Public comments** | Acknowledge in 30 min, move to DM | 30 min ack |
| **Voice calls** | Don't offer in Phase 1 (cost + brand consistency) | n/a |

WhatsApp Business hours: **08:00-18:00 SA time, weekdays.**
Auto-reply outside hours sets expectation, doesn't apologise.

## 4. Decision rules

Use these rules to decide on the spot. Don't escalate everything.

### Refunds

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Within 7 days, any reason | Refund immediately, courier-collect at our cost |
| Defect within 12 months | Replace OR refund (customer picks) |
| Defect after 12 months | Best-effort goodwill; no obligation but consider partial credit |
| Damage from misuse | Apologise, don't refund. Offer 10% off next purchase. |
| Customer claims item never arrived | Check courier proof of delivery first. If POD shows delivered: send POD, ask if neighbour received, escalate to courier. If no POD: full refund OR replacement. |
| Buyer's remorse on day 8-30 | Outside cooling-off window. Decline gently. Offer store credit instead, valid 12 months. |

### Replacements

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Defect verified by photo/video | Ship replacement same day |
| Replacement also defective | Full refund + apology |
| Lost in courier | Replace within 24 hours, claim against courier ourselves |

### Discounts (Phase 1: STRICTLY no discounts)

| Customer asks for | Reply |
|---|---|
| "Any discount available?" | "Not in Phase 1 — we keep one fair price for everyone. Save your discount-energy for the bundle launches in a few months." |
| "Friend & family code?" | "Same answer — fair price for everyone. Send your friend our way and they get the same R499." |

**Why no discounts in Phase 1:** discounting trains customers to wait for
sales. Once that pattern locks in, full-price conversion never recovers.
Hold the line.

## 5. Escalation triggers (founder personally responds within 1 hour)

- Customer publicly tags Nuvanta in a complaint on Twitter / IG / TikTok
- Customer threatens "ombudsman" / "consumer protector" / "Hello Peter"
- Customer says "I'll never buy from you again" or similar
- Repeat complaint about same SKU (3+ unrelated reports → flag SKU for
  quality review)
- Refund request > R750 single order
- Product genuinely caused harm (battery overheating, etc.)

For each, founder replies personally:
1. Within 1 hour
2. Public acknowledge → move to DM
3. Resolve within 24 hours (full refund + sincere apology default position)
4. Log in `/Nuvanta Support Issues` Sheet for trend tracking

## 6. Tone in writing

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Apologies for the inconvenience" | "Sorry — that's frustrating." |
| "Per our policy" | "Here's what we usually do —" |
| "Please be advised" | "Heads up —" |
| "We are unable to" | "We can't, but here's what we can do —" |
| "Have a great day!" | (just sign off, don't perform) |
| Multiple exclamation points!!! | One, max, ever. |
| Capitalised words for emphasis | Italics or quotes if needed. Usually unneeded. |

Read every reply out loud before sending. If it sounds like a corporate
chatbot, rewrite it.

## 7. The 5 questions you'll get most

Pre-think your answers. They'll come up daily.

### 7.1 "How long does delivery take?"
> 1-3 business days metro (Joburg, Cape Town, Durban, Pretoria), 2-5
> business days regional. We dispatch within 2 working days.

### 7.2 "Is this airline-safe?"
> Yes. 74Wh battery is under the 100Wh airline carry-on limit. Take
> it on flights. Keep it in carry-on, not checked.

### 7.3 "Can I pay with EFT/cash on delivery?"
> Card via PayFast or instant EFT via Ozow at checkout. No COD in
> Phase 1 — keeps things simple. Both payment options work on mobile.

### 7.4 "Do you ship outside SA?"
> Not yet — SA-only in Phase 1. We're focused on doing one country
> well first.

### 7.5 "Why R499? It looks like the same one on Takealot for less."
> Honest answer — Takealot has 200+ power banks, including some
> cheaper ones. We tested four of them, picked one we'd actually
> use, and stand behind it with a 12-month warranty + free 7-day
> returns. If price is your main thing, Takealot is fine. If you'd
> rather we picked, we're here.

That last one is the most important. Memorise it. It defines Nuvanta's
positioning every time someone asks.

## 8. Capacity planning

| Daily ticket volume | Action |
|---|---|
| 0-10 | Founder, ~30 min/day |
| 10-30 | Founder, ~1.5 hrs/day, set strict 09:00 + 16:00 reply windows |
| 30-50 | Time to outsource. Hire a part-time SA-based VA, R8-15k/mo. |
| 50+ | Full-time customer support hire. Document this playbook into a hand-off doc. |

Track daily ticket count in the same KPI sheet as ad spend and orders.
The day you cross 30/day reliably, start interviewing VAs the next week.
Don't wait for 50/day — by then you're already drowning.

## 9. End-of-day support routine

15 minutes, every weekday at 17:00:

1. Clear all open WhatsApp chats (reply or close)
2. Clear all orders@ inbox
3. Log any escalations into the support tracker sheet
4. Check Shopify Inbox for missed messages
5. Note any patterns ("3 people asked about airline-safe today" → add
   to homepage FAQ)

Empty inbox at end of day = inbox zero next morning = clear head for
ad creative work.

---

## 10. The only unbreakable rule

**Reply to every customer message. Even the angry ones. Even the weird
ones. Even when you've answered the same question for the third time
that day.**

Silence is what kills small brands in SA. The customer who feels
ignored writes a Hello Peter post that ranks for "Nuvanta" forever.
The customer who feels heard, even if you couldn't fully solve their
issue, becomes a quiet defender of the brand.

Reply.
