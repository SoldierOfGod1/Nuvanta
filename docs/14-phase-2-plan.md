# Phase 2 Plan — Smart Electronics Depth

Generated 2026-05-04. The roadmap for week 8 onwards if Phase 1 wins.

This plan kicks in **only if** Phase 1 hits 30+ orders at CPA <= R150
(see `/docs/10-launch-runbook.md` decision tree). Until then, this doc
sits on the shelf and the founder runs the wedge.

The trap most stores fall into is winning the wedge and immediately
launching 9 SKUs. Don't. Phase 2 is depth in one category before any
breadth. Allbirds shipped only Wool Runners for almost two years before
expanding. Take the lesson.

---

## 1. Phase 2 trigger conditions (all must be true)

- 30+ Shopify orders in the 7-day Phase 1 window
- Final CPA <= R150 across the winning creative + audience
- Refund / return rate < 8%
- Net contribution > R1,000 across the wedge week
- Founder has at least 5 organic customer-feedback emails (from B3 day-7
  flow) suggesting genuine satisfaction
- Trademark + domain ownership confirmed (no pending IP risk)

If any fail, do NOT proceed. Re-run the wedge with a refreshed creative
or pivot SKU instead. False winners are the most expensive lesson in
eCommerce.

---

## 2. Phase 2 goal (90 days)

By the end of week 18:
- 4-6 SKUs live in Smart Electronics
- 200+ paying customers (cumulative since launch)
- Email list: 1,500+ subscribers (purchasers + popup-driven)
- Monthly revenue: R80k-R150k
- Net contribution margin: R20k-R40k/mo
- Repeat purchase rate: 12%+ (low for Phase 2 but realistic)

These numbers are operating targets, not promises. They tell you
whether the brand has gravity or whether you're just running ad budget.

## 4. Bundle strategy (real revenue lever)

Bundles lift AOV without discounting individual SKUs. Three bundles
to launch in week 9-10:

### "The Office Kit" — R899 (save R98)
Power Bank + Multi-port Wall Charger + USB-C Cable 3-pack
Position: "Everything you need to keep your desk powered for a year."

### "The Loadshedding Survival Kit" — R1,649 (save R148)
Power Bank + Mini UPS + Rechargeable LED Desk Lamp + Cable 3-pack
Position: "Stage 6? Doesn't matter. We've got you."

### "The Daily Driver" — R849 (save R98)
Power Bank + Magnetic Car Mount + USB-C Cable 3-pack
Position: "Phone, charged, mounted, ready."

Bundle margins are tighter (~5-8% off vs full price) but lift AOV from
R499 to R849-R1,649. CPA stays roughly the same per *order*. Net
contribution per order roughly doubles.

Don't run "FLASH SALE" framing on bundles. Frame as curation: "We picked
these together because they actually work together."

## 5. Creative pipeline (sustainable cadence)

Phase 1 was 6 creatives in one week — sprint mode. Phase 2 needs a
*sustainable weekly rhythm*. Burn-out kills small brands faster than
bad ads.

### Weekly rhythm (every week of Phase 2)
- **Monday:** Plan the week's shoots (1 hour)
- **Tuesday:** Shoot 4-6 short clips on iPhone (2 hours)
- **Wednesday:** Edit + write copy (3 hours)
- **Thursday:** Upload to ad managers, review with fresh eyes (1 hour)
- **Friday:** Activate. Watch Day 1 performance.
- **Sunday evening:** Review week, prep next week's plan (45 min)

### Creative volume
- 3 new ad creatives per week (was 6 in week 1, now 3 sustained)
- 2 organic TikTok / IG videos per week
- 1 founder-led video per week (Reel + TikTok cross-post)
- 1 email per 2 weeks (broadcasts, not flows)

### Creative angles to test
- Comparison ("we tested 4, this won")
- Problem/solution (specific SA scenarios)
- Before/after (cluttered desk → tidy desk)
- Founder POV (life with the products)
- Customer-led (real Nuvanta buyer demos their kit, with permission)
- Bundle storytelling ("here's the kit" — show the whole bundle in use)

### Founder rule
**One ad never runs longer than 14 days.** Refresh creative weekly.
Ad fatigue compounds fast on Meta + TikTok in SA.

## 6. Ad budget scale path

| Week | Daily spend | Monthly (~30 days) | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8-9 | R600 | R18k | Validate that Phase 1 winner scales |
| 10-12 | R900 | R27k | Stable order flow ~5-10/day |
| 13-15 | R1,200 | R36k | Add second creative angle, test new audiences |
| 16-18 | R1,500 | R45k | Push toward 10-15 orders/day |

**Hard rule:** never scale spend > 1.5x in a single day. Algorithms
need time to recalibrate. 1.5x compounds to 7-8x in 5 days, which is
plenty fast.

**Hard kill rule (still in force):** if daily CPA > R250 sustained for
72 hours, pause spend. Don't hope it'll recover. Diagnose first.

## 7. Email list growth (the most-undervalued lever)

The Phase 1 buyers are the seed list. Goal by week 18: 1,500+ subscribers.

### Sources
- **Buyers** (auto-subscribed with consent): ~5-8/day at scale = ~150/mo
- **Popup (exit-intent only):** 1.5-2.5% of visitors. At 1k visitors/day,
  ~150-250/mo
- **TikTok / IG bio link:** small but qualified, ~30-50/mo
- **Lead magnet (optional, week 12+):** "The 5 things every SA household
  should own to survive Stage 6" — PDF download. Brand-extending.

### Email cadence in Phase 2
- 1 broadcast every 2 weeks
- Always founder-signed
- Mix: 60% product picks, 30% founder thoughts, 10% community / customer stories
- Never daily. Never twice in 7 days. Ever.

## 8. Brand evolution (week 8-18)

Phase 1 was hand-picked-essentials in voice but sparse in execution.
Phase 2 thickens the brand:

- **About page rewrite:** real founder photo, real Sutherland test photos,
  real product-test stories. 500 words, signed.
- **Founder presence on TikTok / IG:** at least 1 founder-face video
  per week. Builds parasocial trust faster than any pixel optimisation.
- **Customer photo wall on site:** 5-10 real Nuvanta customers using
  the gear. Shot by them, their permission. Trust currency.
- **Newsletter cadence (every 2 weeks):** "Here's what we picked this
  week and why."
- **Visual consistency:** every product page uses the same 4-grid photo
  layout (hero, in-context, scale reference, packaging).

The brand starts to look like one human's taste, not an algorithm.

## 9. Logistics & ops at Phase 2 scale

### Trigger thresholds
- **30 orders/day sustained:** outsource WhatsApp support (R8-15k/mo
  SA-based VA)
- **50 orders/day sustained:** outsource order-fulfillment hand-off
  (Dropstore handles, but Q&A / exceptions need someone)
- **100 orders/day sustained:** consider warehousing your top 2 SKUs
  yourself (lower COGS, faster shipping). Decision around month 6.

### Founder protected time
- 4 hours/day on creative + brand + product picks (not ops)
- Calendar block 09:00-13:00, no support, no email
- Support windows: 13:00-14:00, 16:00-17:00 only (after VA hire)

## 11. The Phase 2 founder rule

**No new SKUs after week 14 unless one of the existing 5 underperforms
and gets killed.**

Lock the lineup. Run it. Optimise creative, email, support, brand.
The temptation to keep adding products is the death of curated brands.
Every new SKU dilutes the message and adds operational load. Resist.

The 5 SKUs in Phase 2 should still be the 5 SKUs at week 18 unless
data demanded a swap. That discipline is the brand.
